Guest Speaker

Lauren Pierotti

Associate, Kilpatrick Townsend & Stockton

Lauren Pierotti is an Associate at Kilpatrick Townsend & Stockton, where she advises ETA searchers and investors on the legal dimensions of acquisition transactions. On Search Party, Lauren examines how legal counsel intersects with emotionally charged deal dynamics — particularly how attorneys help searchers navigate the gap between a seller's legacy attachment to their business and the operational realities that a new owner must address. She brings clarity to a part of the deal process that is often misunderstood.

Topics covered on Search Party:
  • How ETA searchers navigate sellers' emotional connection to their businesses
  • Legal considerations in lower middle market acquisitions
  • Managing expectations between sellers and buyers during due diligence
  • Reps and warranties in ETA transactions
  • Common legal pitfalls for first-time acquirers
  • How legal counsel helps smooth the transition of business ownership
  • Structuring deals that work for both buyer and legacy management teams

Welcome to Search Party!

About Search Party

Search Party is a video-podcast dedicated to elevating knowledge of the Entrepreneurship-Through-Acquisition (ETA) model for value creation. The podcast features curated interviews and panel discussions with ETA searchers, CEOs, exited CEOs and investors — covering everything from the search and acquisition process to operations, financing, and exits.

Search Party is hosted by financial journalist David Snow, a long-time chronicler of the alternative investment market.
