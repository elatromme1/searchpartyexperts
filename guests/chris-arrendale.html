Guest Speaker

Chris Arrendale

Founder & Exited CEO, CyberData Pros

Chris Arrendale is the Founder and Exited CEO of CyberData Pros, the cybersecurity firm he built and subsequently sold to fellow ETA operator Keith Burns. As both founder and seller, Chris brings a unique dual perspective to Search Party — he understands the builder's pride in creating a specialized business and the operator's responsibility to ensure it continues to thrive under new ownership. His insights on cybersecurity risks for small businesses are grounded in years of hands-on experience protecting lower middle market companies.

Topics covered on Search Party:
  • Building a cybersecurity services business focused on small and mid-market companies
  • Transitioning leadership as a founder selling to an ETA operator
  • Common cybersecurity vulnerabilities in founder-led businesses
  • Why businesses underestimate their true cyber exposure and risk
  • Cybersecurity certifications that open doors to new revenue opportunities
  • How defense contractors and regulated industries are raising the bar for vendors
  • Protecting customer and client data in lower middle market environments

Welcome to Search Party!

About Search Party

Search Party is a video-podcast dedicated to elevating knowledge of the Entrepreneurship-Through-Acquisition (ETA) model for value creation. The podcast features curated interviews and panel discussions with ETA searchers, CEOs, exited CEOs and investors — covering everything from the search and acquisition process to operations, financing, and exits.

Search Party is hosted by financial journalist David Snow, a long-time chronicler of the alternative investment market.
