Guest Speaker

Burton Francis

Managing Director, ECA Partners

Burton Francis is a Managing Director at ECA Partners, an executive search firm focused on the lower middle market. He brings a talent and leadership lens to the ETA conversation, helping searchers and their investors think about how operational expertise, industry fit, and talent acquisition intersect with long-term value creation. On Search Party, Burton challenges common assumptions about vertical specialization and explains what really distinguishes high-performing ETA operators.

Topics covered on Search Party:
  • Whether ETA searchers should focus on sub-verticals they have worked in
  • How to develop industry fluency quickly as a first-time CEO
  • The importance of pattern recognition in unfamiliar markets
  • Building credibility with legacy management teams
  • Using advisors and mentors to supplement operator expertise
  • What investors look for in talented ETA operators
  • Evaluating executive talent for lower middle market businesses

Welcome to Search Party!

About Search Party

Search Party is a video-podcast dedicated to elevating knowledge of the Entrepreneurship-Through-Acquisition (ETA) model for value creation. The podcast features curated interviews and panel discussions with ETA searchers, CEOs, exited CEOs and investors — covering everything from the search and acquisition process to operations, financing, and exits.

Search Party is hosted by financial journalist David Snow, a long-time chronicler of the alternative investment market.
