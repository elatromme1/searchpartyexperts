Guest Speaker

Daniel Lazier

Co-Founder, Skyline Crest Partners

Daniel Lazier is the Co-Founder of Skyline Crest Partners, an ETA-focused investment firm. On Search Party, Daniel joins a panel with A.J. Wasserstein and Dustin Sellers to examine how ETA investors are genuinely performing in today's market — cutting through the optimism to discuss where returns are being made, where capital has been lost, and how the investor community is adapting to a more competitive and complex deal environment. His practitioner perspective adds ground-level texture to a high-level asset class discussion.

Topics covered on Search Party:
  • Current state of ETA investor returns in a competitive market
  • How to evaluate searcher quality from an investor's vantage point
  • Capital deployment strategy in a market with rising deal prices
  • What distinguishes the best ETA investors from the average
  • Portfolio company challenges that affect investor returns
  • Balancing support for operators with financial discipline as an investor
  • How the ETA ecosystem has matured from an investor's perspective

Welcome to Search Party!

About Search Party

Search Party is a video-podcast dedicated to elevating knowledge of the Entrepreneurship-Through-Acquisition (ETA) model for value creation. The podcast features curated interviews and panel discussions with ETA searchers, CEOs, exited CEOs and investors — covering everything from the search and acquisition process to operations, financing, and exits.

Search Party is hosted by financial journalist David Snow, a long-time chronicler of the alternative investment market.
