Guest Speaker

Keith Burns

CEO, CyberData Pros

Keith Burns is the CEO of CyberData Pros, having acquired the company from its founder after a career acquiring and operating small businesses in the lower middle market. His experience watching cybersecurity become a critical blind spot for ETA-backed operators inspired him to bring CyberData Pros into his portfolio. On Search Party, Keith explains why cybersecurity is no longer a luxury for small businesses — it is a core business protection equivalent to property insurance — and why the AI era has made the threat more immediate and unpredictable than ever.

Topics covered on Search Party:
  • Why lower middle market businesses are increasingly targeted by cyber attacks
  • How AI has dramatically lowered the cost and effort of cybercrime
  • Why newly announced PE-backed acquisitions are frequent attack targets
  • Cybersecurity as a valuation and exit readiness issue
  • What buyers discover in security audits during M&A due diligence
  • The business case for investing in cybersecurity certifications like SOC 2 and CMMC
  • Comparing cybersecurity investment to standard business insurance

About Search Party

Search Party is a video-podcast dedicated to elevating knowledge of the Entrepreneurship-Through-Acquisition (ETA) model for value creation. The podcast features curated interviews and panel discussions with ETA searchers, CEOs, exited CEOs and investors — covering everything from the search and acquisition process to operations, financing, and exits.

Search Party is hosted by financial journalist David Snow, a long-time chronicler of the alternative investment market.
