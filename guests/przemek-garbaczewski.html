Guest Speaker

Przemek Garbaczewski

Co-Founder, Legacy Brothers Capital

Przemek Garbaczewski is a Co-Founder of Legacy Brothers Capital, searching for a lower middle market business to acquire alongside his twin brother Bartosz. On Search Party, Przemek shares their goal of achieving ETA success in the United States before eventually deploying the model in Poland — where entrepreneurship through acquisition remains a relatively new concept with significant untapped potential. Their story represents the increasingly global appeal of the ETA model.

Topics covered on Search Party:
  • Conducting an ETA search in the US as international entrepreneurs
  • The potential for ETA as a value creation model in Poland and Eastern Europe
  • Twin brothers as ETA search partners and the dynamics that creates
  • Identifying acquisition targets in the lower middle market as a new entrant
  • What makes the US ETA ecosystem attractive to European entrepreneurs
  • Building credibility with sellers and investors as a first-generation searcher
  • Long-term vision for deploying ETA learnings across borders

Welcome to Search Party!

About Search Party

Search Party is a video-podcast dedicated to elevating knowledge of the Entrepreneurship-Through-Acquisition (ETA) model for value creation. The podcast features curated interviews and panel discussions with ETA searchers, CEOs, exited CEOs and investors — covering everything from the search and acquisition process to operations, financing, and exits.

Search Party is hosted by financial journalist David Snow, a long-time chronicler of the alternative investment market.
