Guest Speaker

Payal Patel

Senior Associate, Kilpatrick Townsend & Stockton

Payal Patel is a Senior Associate at Kilpatrick Townsend & Stockton, specializing in ETA and search fund transactions. On Search Party, she joins fellow Kilpatrick attorney Lauren Pierotti to explore how the emotional dimension of ETA deals — particularly the deep personal connection sellers have to businesses they have built over decades — shapes negotiation dynamics and deal structuring. Payal helps clients understand that respecting seller legacy is not just good ethics; it is good deal-making.

Topics covered on Search Party:
  • How sellers' emotional attachment to businesses affects deal negotiations
  • Legal strategies for preserving seller legacy during transition
  • Common issues that arise in lower middle market M&A due diligence
  • Structuring earnouts and seller rollover equity
  • Representing both buyers and sellers in search fund transactions
  • The role of legal counsel in managing closing risk
  • Post-close integration considerations from a legal standpoint

Welcome to Search Party!

About Search Party

Search Party is a video-podcast dedicated to elevating knowledge of the Entrepreneurship-Through-Acquisition (ETA) model for value creation. The podcast features curated interviews and panel discussions with ETA searchers, CEOs, exited CEOs and investors — covering everything from the search and acquisition process to operations, financing, and exits.

Search Party is hosted by financial journalist David Snow, a long-time chronicler of the alternative investment market.
