Guest Speaker

Casey Schuler

CEO, PalCare

Casey Schuler is the CEO of PalCare, a healthcare technology platform that powers nurse-call and emergency alert systems for assisted living and memory care communities. Casey and his business school friend Hutson Prioleau acquired PalCare in late 2019 — just months before COVID-19 shut down senior living facilities across the country. On Search Party, Casey shares how he and Hutson survived an 80% revenue halt, cut costs aggressively, automated their systems, and emerged as a stronger, more resilient company. His story is one of the most vivid illustrations of ETA ownership under pressure.

Topics covered on Search Party:
  • Navigating a major COVID-19 business crisis within months of acquisition close
  • The power of partnered search and complementary skill sets between co-CEOs
  • How a shared trust between partners enables faster, tougher decisions
  • Rebuilding revenue through operational pivots and new go-to-market strategies
  • Transitioning from survival mode to a growth and scale mindset
  • Building a COO and CRO leadership layer after years of founder-operated growth
  • Why choosing your industry matters as much as finding a good deal

About Search Party

Search Party is a video-podcast dedicated to elevating knowledge of the Entrepreneurship-Through-Acquisition (ETA) model for value creation. The podcast features curated interviews and panel discussions with ETA searchers, CEOs, exited CEOs and investors — covering everything from the search and acquisition process to operations, financing, and exits.

Search Party is hosted by financial journalist David Snow, a long-time chronicler of the alternative investment market.
