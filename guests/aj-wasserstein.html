Guest Speaker

A.J. Wasserstein

Senior Lecturer in the Practice of Management, Yale School of Management

A.J. Wasserstein is a Senior Lecturer at the Yale School of Management, where he teaches entrepreneurship through acquisition and is one of the foremost academic authorities on the ETA model. A former searcher himself, A.J. brings rigorous data and practitioner credibility to his analysis of how the search fund ecosystem is evolving. On Search Party, he joins a panel discussion on how ETA investors are faring in the current environment, offering honest assessments of what the data says about returns, risks, and the state of the market.

Topics covered on Search Party:
  • Academic research on ETA search fund returns and performance trends
  • How the ETA investor community is performing in today's market
  • What the data reveals about search fund success rates and failure modes
  • Historical context for the current wave of ETA deal activity
  • What MBA programs are teaching the next generation of ETA operators
  • The evolution of the search fund model from niche strategy to mainstream asset class
  • How experienced investors and new entrants are approaching ETA differently

Welcome to Search Party!

About Search Party

Search Party is a video-podcast dedicated to elevating knowledge of the Entrepreneurship-Through-Acquisition (ETA) model for value creation. The podcast features curated interviews and panel discussions with ETA searchers, CEOs, exited CEOs and investors — covering everything from the search and acquisition process to operations, financing, and exits.

Search Party is hosted by financial journalist David Snow, a long-time chronicler of the alternative investment market.
