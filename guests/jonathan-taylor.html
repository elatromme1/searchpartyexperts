Guest Speaker

Jonathan Taylor

CEO, AEK Technology

Jonathan Taylor is the CEO of AEK Technology, a small defense contractor that has been navigating the increasingly rigorous cybersecurity certification requirements imposed by government customers. On Search Party, Jonathan offers a customer's perspective on what cybersecurity investment really means at the small business level — not as a tech expense, but as a prerequisite for winning contracts and preserving revenue. His firsthand account of CMMC compliance illuminates the real-world stakes for lower middle market operators in regulated industries.

Topics covered on Search Party:
  • Why government contractors must invest in cybersecurity certifications to compete
  • Cmmc compliance and what it means for small defense businesses
  • How failing to meet cyber standards costs companies real revenue opportunities
  • The practical experience of pursuing SOC 2 and ISO 27001 certifications
  • Balancing cybersecurity investment with the financial constraints of small businesses
  • How cyber requirements are cascading down from large primes to small vendors
  • Working with specialized cybersecurity partners as a small operator

Welcome to Search Party!

About Search Party

Search Party is a video-podcast dedicated to elevating knowledge of the Entrepreneurship-Through-Acquisition (ETA) model for value creation. The podcast features curated interviews and panel discussions with ETA searchers, CEOs, exited CEOs and investors — covering everything from the search and acquisition process to operations, financing, and exits.

Search Party is hosted by financial journalist David Snow, a long-time chronicler of the alternative investment market.
