Guest Speaker

Dustin Sellers

Managing Partner, NCL Partners

Dustin Sellers is a Managing Partner at NCL Partners (Next Coast Legacy), one of the most active ETA search fund investors in the market. A former searcher himself, Dustin is one of Search Party's most frequent guests and panel participants, offering an investor's perspective on everything from deal sizing and search strategy to operator compensation and exit markets. His candid commentary on what separates successful ETA searchers from the rest makes him a go-to voice in the ecosystem.

Topics covered on Search Party:
  • How ETA search is entering a new era of larger and more complex deals
  • Ncl's criteria for evaluating prospective searchers
  • Executive compensation design tied to EBITDA performance
  • Current exit market conditions for ETA-backed businesses
  • The case for partnered search versus solo search
  • When ETA investors are really faring in today's market
  • Value creation playbooks for lower middle market CEOs

Welcome to Search Party!

About Search Party

Search Party is a video-podcast dedicated to elevating knowledge of the Entrepreneurship-Through-Acquisition (ETA) model for value creation. The podcast features curated interviews and panel discussions with ETA searchers, CEOs, exited CEOs and investors — covering everything from the search and acquisition process to operations, financing, and exits.

Search Party is hosted by financial journalist David Snow, a long-time chronicler of the alternative investment market.
