Guest Speaker

August Felker

CEO & Founder, Oberle Risk Strategies

August Felker is the CEO and Founder of Oberle Risk Strategies, a Search Party sponsor and one of the ETA ecosystem's most trusted insurance advisors. Having completed his own ETA search and acquisition of an insurance brokerage before founding Oberle, August brings the rare perspective of a searcher-turned-operator-turned-specialist. On Search Party, he walks through the insurance essentials every lower middle market acquirer must understand — from need-to-have coverages to the risks that catch new CEOs off guard.

Topics covered on Search Party:
  • Insurance essentials for lower middle market business acquisitions
  • Why acquired businesses are often dangerously underinsured
  • Need-to-have versus nice-to-have insurance policies for ETA CEOs
  • Cybersecurity insurance for small and mid-sized businesses
  • Directors and officers insurance in investor-backed companies
  • Professional liability and healthcare industry insurance risks
  • How insurance diligence reveals hidden risks in target businesses

Welcome to Search Party!

About Search Party

Search Party is a video-podcast dedicated to elevating knowledge of the Entrepreneurship-Through-Acquisition (ETA) model for value creation. The podcast features curated interviews and panel discussions with ETA searchers, CEOs, exited CEOs and investors — covering everything from the search and acquisition process to operations, financing, and exits.

Search Party is hosted by financial journalist David Snow, a long-time chronicler of the alternative investment market.
