Guest Speaker

Nathan Faith

Wealth Partner, Boulay

Nathan Faith is a Wealth Partner at Boulay, a firm that specializes in serving ETA searchers and small business owners with integrated financial planning. He is one of Search Party's most frequent guests, bringing practical tax, estate, and wealth-planning insights to entrepreneurs navigating the acquisition and ownership lifecycle. From residency planning to equity rollover decisions, Nathan explains how smart financial structuring can meaningfully change outcomes for searcher-CEOs.

Topics covered on Search Party:
  • Tax advantages of changing residency ahead of an acquisition
  • How much equity sellers should roll into an acquiring company
  • Whether searcher-CEOs should take out credit lines against equity value
  • Role of trusts in an ETA searcher's exit plan
  • Business sale tax strategies and timing
  • Investment account considerations for ETA operators
  • Wealth planning for first-time CEOs post-acquisition

Welcome to Search Party!

About Search Party

Search Party is a video-podcast dedicated to elevating knowledge of the Entrepreneurship-Through-Acquisition (ETA) model for value creation. The podcast features curated interviews and panel discussions with ETA searchers, CEOs, exited CEOs and investors — covering everything from the search and acquisition process to operations, financing, and exits.

Search Party is hosted by financial journalist David Snow, a long-time chronicler of the alternative investment market.
