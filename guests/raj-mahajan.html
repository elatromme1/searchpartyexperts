Guest Speaker

Raj Mahajan

Founder & CEO, Jaan Capital

Raj Mahajan is the Founder and CEO of Jaan Capital, a second-time entrepreneur-through-acquisition who has significantly evolved his approach after his first ETA experience. On Search Party, Raj shares candid lessons from his first acquisition and explains how a second-time buyer brings sharper deal discipline, stronger operator intuition, and a clearer vision of value creation. His journey illustrates how the ETA model rewards those willing to iterate and go again.

Topics covered on Search Party:
  • Lessons from a first ETA acquisition applied to a second search
  • What second-time searchers do differently in due diligence
  • Building conviction around a target business
  • Navigating investor relationships as a repeat searcher
  • Operational improvements that create measurable EBITDA gains
  • How the ETA community evolves with experience

Welcome to Search Party!

About Search Party

Search Party is a video-podcast dedicated to elevating knowledge of the Entrepreneurship-Through-Acquisition (ETA) model for value creation. The podcast features curated interviews and panel discussions with ETA searchers, CEOs, exited CEOs and investors — covering everything from the search and acquisition process to operations, financing, and exits.

Search Party is hosted by financial journalist David Snow, a long-time chronicler of the alternative investment market.
